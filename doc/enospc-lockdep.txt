The lockdep report a full volume USED TO produce, captured in full.

THIS IS A RECORD OF A FIXED DEFECT, kept because it is the evidence the
fix was made against and because the two orders in it are worth reading.
The cause was hammer2_chain_create() clearing a caller's chain pointer on
the ENOSPC path and so skipping that caller's release; twenty-five runs
whose logs are kept have filled a volume since without reporting a cycle.
A run today that produces anything resembling this is a regression, not
a reproduction.


Measured on artix-s6-kde at 7.3.0-rc1 with CONFIG_PROVE_LOCKING, on a 2 GiB
volume, by script/enospc.sh. 583 files of 4 MiB were written before dd
reported ENOSPC; debug_locks was 1 after the fill and 0 after the sync(2)
that followed. One report, counted by banner occurrences.

Streamed out of /dev/kmsg for the whole run and printed before the unmount:
the ring wraps before a fill ends, and the unmount hangs on most of these
runs and takes the log with it. The kmsg facility and sequence prefixes are
stripped here and nothing else is edited. doc/README.status.md reads it.
