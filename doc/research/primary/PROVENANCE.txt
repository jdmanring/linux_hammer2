Primary sources for the HAMMER2 cache-coherency (CCMS) work, captured so
that no reading in doc/CCMS_REFERENCE.md has to be re-fetched or
re-derived from a shallow clone.

Everything here was captured 2026-10-10. Each entry names the command
or reader that produced it. Files are named for the document; the
"deleted" ones are the revision before commit 94491fa098, which is where
the cache-coherency half of HAMMER2 lives.

Not captured, and why
=====================
Pacemaker "Fencing" and Red Hat "Configuring Fencing using SCSI
Persistent Reservations" are the only survey sources not kept here. Both
describe the fencing mechanism GPFS invokes rather than a design, and
neither was needed to verify a claim in doc/CCMS_REFERENCE.md; their URLs
are in that file's survey section.

Colossus's lease details are not published. The survey's claim about it is
the sharded metadata service, from the Google Cloud blog post "A peek
behind Colossus, Google's file system" (2021-04-19).
